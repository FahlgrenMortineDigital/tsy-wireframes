See & Do · Outdoor Adventures

Kayak, paddleboard, hike, fish.

487 acres of preserve. Mangrove tunnels at low tide. Charters that put you on tarpon at sunrise. The outdoor side of Florida's West Coast — quieter than you'd expect, wilder than the brochures show.

ROBINSON · MANGROVE TUNNEL
Why outdoor here

A coastline still wild enough to be worth visiting.

Most of Florida's coast got built. We protected ours. Robinson Preserve alone is 487 acres of restored coastal habitat — bird rookeries, mangrove tunnels, kayak trails, and trail markers locals still get wrong.

Accessibility

Boardwalks, beach wheelchairs, accessible kayak launches.

Robinson Preserve has paved paths and observation towers with ramps. Manatee County loans beach wheelchairs at multiple parks. Adaptive kayak programs are available — see the accessibility planning guide for details.
