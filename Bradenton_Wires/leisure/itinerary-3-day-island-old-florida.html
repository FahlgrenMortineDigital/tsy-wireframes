Island Beaches, Old Florida & Downtown Bradenton.

The crowd-tested loop for first-time visitors who want a beach week without missing the heritage, the food, or the river. Three days, three communities, no rental car required.

Day 01 · Arrival

Anna Maria Island & Sunset on Bridge Street.

Land at SRQ, drive 25 minutes, park at Coquina Beach for the day. Take the trolley north to Pine Avenue. Walk, eat, swim, sunset, dinner.

MorningArrive · drop bags · drive to Coquina Beach
LunchBridge Street walk-up tacos
AfternoonTrolley to Pine Avenue · Bean Point Beach
SunsetBean Point or the Anna Maria City Pier
DinnerThe Sandbar · toes in the sand
Day 1 · Featured stop

Anna Maria Island

Three small cities on a seven-mile barrier island.
Where to stay

The Waterline Marina Resort

Bayfront boutique, walk to Bridge Street.
Day 02 · Heritage & Nature

Cortez Seafood, Robinson Preserve & Ferry to Riverwalk.

Drive 10 minutes to Cortez for a working-village morning. Kayak Robinson Preserve. Catch the afternoon ferry to downtown Bradenton.

MorningCortez Village · breakfast · Maritime Museum
Late morningRobinson Preserve guided kayak tour
LunchStar Fish Company · picnic on the dock
Afternoon3pm ferry · Bridge Street → Riverwalk
DinnerPier 22 · riverfront table
Day 2 · Featured stop

Cortez Village

Florida's last working fishing village.
Day 2 · Transit

Gulf Islands Ferry

3pm departure · Bridge Street → Riverwalk.
Day 03 · Downtown & Departure

Bishop Museum, Riverwalk Brunch & Longboat Afternoon.

A slower morning downtown. Drive to Longboat Key for a quiet beach afternoon before flying out.

MorningBishop Museum of Science & Nature
BrunchPier 22 or Birdrock Taco Shack
AfternoonDrive to Longboat Key · St. Regis beach club
Late afternoonBeach time · pack · drive to SRQ
DepartureSRQ · 25 minutes from Longboat
Day 3 · Featured stop

Bishop Museum

Planetarium, manatee viewing, riverfront.
Day 3 · Bonus stay

St. Regis Longboat Key

Extend a day if you can — the beach is the show.
