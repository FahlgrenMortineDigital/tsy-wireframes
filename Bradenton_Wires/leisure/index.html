Gulf Islands Ferry

Park once. Ride the water. Explore the island.

Skip the bridge traffic and let the Manatee do the work. The ferry runs from the Bradenton Riverwalk to Bridge Street — a 45-minute crossing that's part of the trip, not a chore on the way to it.
