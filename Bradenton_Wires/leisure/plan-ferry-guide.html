Gulf Islands Ferry

Park once. Ride the water. Explore the island.

A 45-minute ride between Bradenton's Riverwalk and Bridge Street on Anna Maria Island. The ferry isn't just a way to skip the bridge — it's part of the trip.

Ferry FAQ

Common questions.

How long is the ride?

About 45 minutes from dock to dock. Sit on the upper deck for the best view.

Are bikes & strollers allowed?

Yes — bikes ride free, strollers fold flat in the lower cabin.

Is the ferry pet-friendly?

Leashed dogs are welcome on the lower deck.

What if it's bad weather?

Service continues in light rain. Lightning or 25+ knot winds will delay or cancel — refunds are automatic.

Where do I buy tickets?

Online (recommended), at the Riverwalk dock, or onboard if seats are available.

Wheelchair accessible?

Yes — boarding ramp, accessible seating, and accessible restroom onboard.
