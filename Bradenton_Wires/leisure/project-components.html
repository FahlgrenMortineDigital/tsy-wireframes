Component 22
FAQ Accordion
Back to top ↑
BGI LeisureModule

Native <details>-based accordion. Open / closed indicator on the right.

Do I need a car?

Not always. The Gulf Islands Ferry, the Anna Maria trolley, and a tight downtown make car-light trips genuinely doable.

What's the best beach for families?

Coquina Beach in Bradenton Beach for facilities; Manatee Public Beach in Holmes Beach for a more local feel.

What is "Floribbean" and where do I eat it?

The fusion of Floridian and Caribbean flavors built around fresh local seafood. Cortez Village and Bridge Street are the best places to start.
