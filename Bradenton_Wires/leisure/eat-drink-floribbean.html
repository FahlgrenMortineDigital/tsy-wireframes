Eat & Drink · Floribbean

Caught this morning. Plated tonight.

Floribbean isn't a marketing term. It's how this coast has been eating for generations — Floridian seafood with a Caribbean kitchen, plated within sight of the dock the catch came off.

FLORIBBEAN HERO
What is Floribbean?

Florida coast meets Caribbean kitchen — on a plate.

Mahi-mahi blackened over jerk rub. Conch fritters with mango aioli. Hogfish you watched come off the boat. Key lime pie that tastes like the trees are still on the property. Floribbean is what happens when island spices meet local seafood and zero pretense.

Cortez Village is where you go to see how it's caught. Bridge Street and Anna Maria are where you go to taste it.
