About Anna Maria Island

Florida as it once was — preserved on a barrier island.

Three small cities — Anna Maria, Holmes Beach, and Bradenton Beach — share one seven-mile-long island connected to the mainland by two bridges. Building heights are capped, chains are kept out, and the rhythm of the place is set by the tide and the trolley schedule.

The locals call this "Real. Authentic. Florida.™" because that's exactly what it is: low-rise, low-key, and the kind of place where the bartender knows your beach order by Tuesday.

Getting around

Park once. Ride the trolley. Skip the bridge.

The free Anna Maria Island Trolley runs end-to-end every 20 minutes. The Gulf Islands Ferry runs from Bradenton Riverwalk to Bridge Street year-round — the easiest way onto the island when summer traffic peaks.
