About the Bradenton Area

Florida as it once was — preserved on Florida's West Coast.

We are Florida as it once was, before the age of amusement parks and expansive resorts: pure natural beauty, preserved for all to enjoy and for generations to come. Located on Florida's West Coast, the destination is known for its unspoiled beaches, rich history, the easy pace of island life, and a downtown energized with arts, culture, and discoveries.

Our destination comprises three primary locations: Bradenton, Anna Maria Island, and Longboat Key — and the communities that surround them.

Frequently asked

Questions, answered for the way you actually plan.

How is the Bradenton Area different from Sarasota or St. Pete-Clearwater?

Lower-rise, less developed, and more nature-forward. We're the Florida coast you came looking for before the high-rises got there.

Do I need a car?

Not always. The Gulf Islands Ferry, the Anna Maria trolley, and a tight downtown make car-light trips genuinely doable.

What's the best beach for families?

Coquina Beach in Bradenton Beach for facilities and parking; Manatee Public Beach in Holmes Beach for a more local feel.

What is "Floribbean" and where do I eat it?

The fusion of Floridian and Caribbean flavors built around fresh local seafood. Cortez Village and Bridge Street are the best places to start.

What about meetings and conventions?

The Bradenton Area Convention Center has its own site at meetings.bacvb.com with full RFP and venue details.
