About Cortez

Family-run boats. Family-run fish houses. Family-run for 137 years.

Cortez has been a working commercial fishing village since 1888. The same families still run the boats, the fish houses, and the small handful of restaurants that have made the village a destination for visitors who want their seafood plated within sight of the dock it came off.

It's listed on the National Register of Historic Places. It also has the best stone crab in the state.

What Cortez is for.

Order at the window, eat at a picnic table on the dock. The seafood was swimming this morning.

Heritage

Florida Maritime Museum

The story of the village in a 1912 schoolhouse — boats, nets, photos, and the families that run them.

Outdoor

Charter Fishing

Half-day inshore charters with local captains. Tarpon in summer, snook year-round.

Festival

Cortez Stone Crab Festival

Two days every February — stone crabs, music, and the village on full display.

No-frills sand, dunes, sunsets. Walk-on, low-traffic, low-key. Just over the bridge.
