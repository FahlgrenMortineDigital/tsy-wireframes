Common questions

The quick answers.

Do I need to rent a car?

It depends on where you stay. Anna Maria + downtown is doable car-light using the ferry and trolley. Lakewood Ranch and Longboat Key effectively require a car.

Which airport should I fly into?

SRQ if you can — closest, easiest. TPA for more options. PIE for cheaper fares.

When is the best time to visit?

March–May: warm, dry, peak. October–November: warm, quiet, locals' favorite. June–September: hot but the beaches breathe.

How does the Ferry Perks Pass work?

Round-trip pass plus discounts at restaurants near both ferry stops. Pays for itself in two stops.
