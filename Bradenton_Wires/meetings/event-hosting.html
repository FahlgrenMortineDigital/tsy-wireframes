POWEL CROSLEY ESTATE · 1929
Off-site venue

Powel Crosley Estate.

A mere 10 miles from the Bradenton Area Convention Center, the historic Powel Crosley Estate was once the summer home of legendary innovator and broadcast pioneer Powel Crosley Jr. Today, it's the destination's most distinctive off-site for high-touch board dinners, executive retreats, and welcome receptions.
