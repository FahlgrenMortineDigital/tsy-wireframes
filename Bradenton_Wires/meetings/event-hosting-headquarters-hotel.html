PALMETTO MARRIOTT · RIVERFRONT
About the property

Connected campus. Riverfront. Built for groups.

The Palmetto Marriott Resort & Spa serves as the destination's headquarters hotel for meetings and events. Located on the Bradenton Area Convention Center campus on the Manatee River, the resort offers 266 guest rooms, 20,000 sq ft of additional flexible meeting space, an on-site spa and golf, and direct walking access to the BACC.

For groups under 500, the hotel functions as a self-contained venue. For larger programs, it pairs with the BACC for general session and reception, with overflow blocks at properties under ten minutes away.
