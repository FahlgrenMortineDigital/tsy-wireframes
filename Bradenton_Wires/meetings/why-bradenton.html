Why host here

Real. Authentic. Florida.™ — for groups.

The Bradenton Area is one of the few places in the state where groups can still experience Real. Authentic. Florida.™ — known for its natural beauty, easy island pace, and a destination your attendees will rebook themselves into.

In this section Location Dining Things To Do Meet the Team Why Bradenton
Location

25 minutes from SRQ. 60 from Tampa. Right on the Manatee River.

Sarasota–Bradenton International (SRQ) gives your attendees 30+ nonstop options. Tampa International (TPA) and St. Pete-Clearwater (PIE) offer overflow — both within an hour. The Convention Center sits riverside in Palmetto, with a connected headquarters Marriott and 1,500 rooms within ten minutes.

Dining

Floribbean cuisine at the table you reserved.

Delighting a variety of palates is easy with our fusion of Floridian and Caribbean flavors — local seafood (often caught that day) and fresh, seasonal produce. Whether it's a 12-person board dinner or a 400-person dine-around, we have the relationships and the maps already drawn.
