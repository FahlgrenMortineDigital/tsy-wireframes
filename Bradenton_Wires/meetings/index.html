Why the Bradenton Area

Real venues. Real beaches. No commute between them.

The Bradenton Area is one of the few places in the state where groups can still experience Real. Authentic. Florida.™ — and where a meeting day on the river ends with toes-in-the-sand the same evening.

01 · Location

25 minutes from SRQ.

Sarasota–Bradenton International with 30+ nonstops. Tampa and St. Pete-Clearwater inside an hour.

02 · Venue

Riverside, full-service convention center.

Flexible 60,000 sq ft on the Manatee River, in-house catering by Gulf Islands Hospitality.

03 · Destination

Beaches, ferry, and Bridge Street, in 20 minutes.

Pre/post stay opportunities your attendees will book themselves.

CONVENTION CENTER · MANATEE RIVER
The venue

Reimagined spaces. Unforgettable meetings.

The Bradenton Area Convention Center is a state-of-the-art facility designed to host a variety of events, from large conventions to intimate banquets. Located on the scenic Manatee River, the BACC is a flexible full-service meeting space with the resources and the staff necessary to make your event a resounding success.
