CROSLEY ESTATE · BAYFRONT
About the estate

A historic Florida bayfront — for the night that gets remembered.

The Powel Crosley Estate is a 21,000 sq ft Mediterranean Revival mansion built in 1929 as a winter retreat for innovator and broadcast pioneer Powel Crosley, Jr. Today the estate is owned by Manatee County and operated as a public historic site, available for private events.

Used by planners for board dinners, sponsor receptions, executive retreats, and welcome events that need to feel different from the convention center. Mainland location, ten miles from the BACC, twenty-five from SRQ.
