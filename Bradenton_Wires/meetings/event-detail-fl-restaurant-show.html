About the show

Florida's largest hospitality industry trade show.

Now in its 19th year, the Florida Restaurant & Lodging Show brings together independent operators, multi-unit chains, hotel groups, and the suppliers who serve them. Two days of exhibits, education sessions, and the state's largest culinary stage — all in one ballroom under one roof.

The show is organized by the Florida Restaurant & Lodging Association. Industry registration is required.

Culinary Stage

Live demos from 18 of Florida's best chefs.

Two days of live cooking demos, panels, and tastings on the largest culinary stage at any Florida hospitality show. This year's lineup features chefs from Tampa, Miami, Sarasota, and across the West Coast — including a Floribbean showcase from Gulf Islands Hospitality, the in-house caterer at the BACC.

Registration

Industry registration only.

The Florida Restaurant & Lodging Show is an industry-only event. Attendees must be active in the restaurant, lodging, hospitality, food service, or supplier industries. ID and credentials verified at registration.

  • Operator passFree for verified operators (restaurants, hotels, food service)
