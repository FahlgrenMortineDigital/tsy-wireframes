Component 13a
Text Block
Back to top

Body copy block — H4 subheading + one or more paragraphs. The component the live FK site (e.g. /us/our-impact/improving-access) uses for long-form content sections. Sourced from Figma Text Block (3832:8682). Use this for substantive prose; reserve Callout for emphasized highlights.

A focus on generic injectables and biosimilars

Our focus in Pharma has always been in generic injectables, and now, as we expand in Biopharma, we are also specializing in biosimilars. According to the Association for Accessible Medicines, FDA-approved generic and biosimilar medicines represent 90% of prescriptions dispensed in the U.S. but only 13.1% of total drug spending and just 1.2% of total U.S. health care spending. Their use created $445 billion in savings in 2023 for patients and the U.S. health care system.

At Fresenius Kabi, our teams gladly shoulder the responsibility of our essential role in driving affordability in healthcare. Every day, we find ways to offer more than 170 of these IV drugs at accessible costs.

Clinical Definition

Parenteral nutrition is the intravenous administration of a formulated solution containing macronutrients (dextrose, amino acids, lipid emulsion) and micronutrients (electrolytes, vitamins, trace elements) to maintain or restore nutritional status when the enteral route is not feasible, adequate, or safe.

Not Defined in Figma · Variant of Callout (13c)
Spotlight Callout
Back to top

Wide centered emphasis block — eyebrow + bold title + body — on a light-grey rounded background. Different from Callout (13c): full width, no left rule, larger title, centered alignment. Used to surface a key clinical claim or guideline change mid-page (e.g. oncology "Lipid Considerations", critical-care "Guideline Update"). Not yet in the Figma library — expressed via .content-spotlight.

Guideline update

PN can start earlier in the ICU course than guidelines once allowed.

The historical practice of withholding PN for the first seven days has been revised. Emerging evidence demonstrates that early supplemental PN — when enteral nutrition alone is inadequate — may reduce caloric deficits and preserve lean body mass without increasing complication rates.

Not Defined in Figma · Layout pattern
Process Flow
Back to top

Vertical numbered process — large faint number on the left, title + body on the right, separated by hairline rules. Used for ordered clinical sequences (e.g. malnutrition 5-step refeeding protocol, critical-care 3-phase dosing, patient-selection-criteria 4-question framework). Not yet in the Figma library — expressed via .content-flow.

01

Acute phase — Days 1–3

Hypocaloric feeding at 15–20 kcal/kg, protein 1.2–1.5 g/kg, lipid ≤0.8 g/kg. Avoid overfeeding; monitor glucose closely.

02

Early recovery — Days 4–7

Energy 20–25 kcal/kg, protein 1.5–2.0 g/kg, lipid 0.8–1.0 g/kg. Advance toward goal as the inflammatory response subsides.

03

Late recovery — Week 2+

Full caloric targets at 25–30 kcal/kg. Optimize protein for rehabilitation; transition to enteral or oral nutrition as soon as feasible.
