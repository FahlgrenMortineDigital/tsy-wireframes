1. Executive Summary

Wireframe-to-Build Analysis & Efficiency Assessment

The columbusregion.com WordPress site contains 38 individual UI components across 50+ pages. After analysis and consolidation of variants, these map to approximately 22 development components for the Statamic rebuild.

Two existing Statamic sites — greenbrierwv.com and visitknoxohio.org — provide significant component leverage. Of the 22 dev components, 13 have direct Statamic precedent (59%), 5 have partial precedent (23%), and only 4 require net-new builds (18%).

9. Key Consolidation Decisions

Rationale for component merges

Heroes (3 → 1): Full-width overlay, half-width block, and counties hero are all variants of one hero component with configurable panel position, color, and optional form/dropdown.

Accordions (3 → 1): Dark BG (Industries), Light BG (Operations), and FAQ are one component with theme toggle, optional paired image, and optional stats panel.

Content Blocks (2 → 1): Alternating Content and Two-Column Content are one component with an image_position field.

Stats (2 → 1): Stats Panel and Stats Callout Bar are one component with a layout variant (inline vs. centered).

Contact CTAs (2 → 1): Light Background and Image Overlay are one component with a variant toggle.

News Cards (3 → 1): News Card, Press Release Card, and News Feed Card are one card partial with show_image and layout options.

Counties (3 → 1): County Snapshot, City Card, and County Tab Bar merge into one County system powered by the Counties collection.

Newsletter (2 → 1): Signup Bar and Inline Email merge into one component with layout variants.

10. Risk Factors

Items requiring early attention or client decisions

⚠ Interactive County Map (D12) — 16–20 hours

Custom SVG with 11 county paths, hover states, click navigation. No Statamic precedent. Prioritize early in sprint to de-risk. Consider: could a clickable grid of county cards replace the interactive map?

⚠ County Tab System (D15) — 14–18 hours

11-county tab bar + dynamic snapshot panel + optional map integration. Complex data relationships between counties, cities, and employers.

● iFrame Audit (Economy Page)

19 iframes detected on Economy page. Many may be outdated embeds. Audit before migration — replacing with native components could improve performance significantly.

● Weglot Translation

Current site supports 6 languages. Neither reference site uses translation. Confirm with client if multilingual support is still required.

● Project Announcements Filter

Multi-faceted filtering (year, type, jobs, facility, location). Most complex collection query. Evaluate if Statamic's native filtering + Alpine.js is sufficient or if Algolia/search integration is needed.

✔ Recommendation

Proceed with the 160–200 hour estimate. Prioritize the Interactive Map (D12) and County System (D15) in Sprint 1. The 13 heavy-leverage components represent 59% of the build and can be ported quickly from Greenbrier/Knox codebases.
