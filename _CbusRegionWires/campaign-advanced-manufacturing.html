1 Campaign Header — logo + single CTA, no site navigation
Landing-page conversion strategy
  • Standalone paid-campaign page at campaigns.columbusregion.com/advanced-manufacturing — served on the campaign subdomain, not linked from any site navigation, and set noindex so paid traffic (search/social/display ads targeting site selectors) lands here with tight message-match.
  • Single conversion goal: every CTA on the page funnels to one action — “Connect with an expert” (the lead form). No competing links, no mega-menu, no footer sitemap — landing-page best practice is to remove exit points that leak the click.
  • Above-the-fold form: the primary lead form sits in the hero so a qualified visitor can convert without scrolling; the sticky bottom bar + a repeated form at the base capture everyone else.
  • Persuasion order (proven LP structure): benefit headline → form → value prop → hard proof (stats) → sector depth → brand-name proof (employers) → testimonial → final CTA + reassurance.
  • Content source: live campaign page campaigns.columbusregion.com/advanced-manufacturing (One Columbus). Stats, sectors, employer logos, and the testimonial are pulled from that page; the headline is optimized for ad message-match. All copy is newly written for this template.
Campaign Header — new component (c-lp-header) · Light build (1–2 hrs)
  • Build: a stripped Statamic entry type campaign_landing with its own minimal layout — logo (Global asset) links to home in a new tab only; a single phone/CTA on the right. Deliberately omits c1 utility bar, c2/c3/c4 nav + drawer, and the full c5 footer.
  • Message match: H1, hero image, and form heading should echo the ad creative and keyword (e.g. “advanced manufacturing site selection”) — editable per campaign so Marketing can spin up variants (Automotive & Mobility, Logistics & eCommerce, Life Sciences) from the same template.
  • Analytics: GA4 + conversion pixel on form submit; sticky-CTA and hero-CTA as separate events to attribute which converts.
