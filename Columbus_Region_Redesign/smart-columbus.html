Navigation — WCAG 2.2 AA build requirements
  • 2.1.1 Keyboard (A): each top-level item is a <button aria-expanded> — opens/closes on click, Enter or Space.
  • 1.4.13 Content on Hover or Focus (AA): the panel is Dismissible (Esc closes & returns focus to the trigger), Hoverable (no gap between trigger and panel), and Persistent.
  • 4.1.2 Name, Role, Value (A): aria-expanded reflects open state; aria-controls points to the panel id.
  • 2.4.7 Focus Visible (AA): visible focus ring on the trigger and every sub-link.
  • 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (AA — new in 2.2): the sticky header must not hide a focused link (apply scroll-margin-top).
  • 2.5.8 Target Size Minimum (AA — new in 2.2): triggers & links are ≥ 24×24 px.
  • Search drawer: toggle is a <button aria-expanded aria-controls>; focus moves to the field on open, Esc closes and returns focus to the toggle, backdrop click dismisses.
Hover still opens the menu as a convenience — keyboard, Esc, and click-outside are all supported. Toggle this note via “Accessibility (WCAG)” in Wireframe Controls.

Brand Architecture

The Region’s Innovation Lab

Smart Columbus Image

Smart Columbus is the innovation lab of the Columbus Partnership, and it is the public-facing side of the region’s smart city work. Where One Columbus serves companies and site consultants, Smart Columbus serves residents — piloting mobility, energy, and technology solutions that make daily life in central Ohio work better for everyone.

It grew out of the region’s 2016 win in the U.S. Department of Transportation Smart City Challenge, a national competition the Columbus Region won outright. What began as a federal grant has become a standing lab that tests new ideas in the real world, proves what works, and scales the successes into lasting public benefit.

Within the Partnership’s brand architecture, that gives the region three complementary roles: the Partnership sets the agenda, One Columbus grows the economy, and Smart Columbus improves the experience of living here.

Common Questions

Smart Columbus, Explained

What is Smart Columbus?

Smart Columbus is the innovation lab of the Columbus Partnership and the region’s smart city effort. It pilots mobility, clean-energy, and technology solutions focused on residents and the everyday experience of living in central Ohio.

Is Columbus a smart city?

Yes. The Columbus Region won the U.S. Department of Transportation’s national Smart City Challenge in 2016, and Smart Columbus has carried that work forward ever since — testing connected transportation, electric mobility, and digital-inclusion programs across the community.

How is Smart Columbus different from One Columbus?

Both are part of the Columbus Partnership. One Columbus is the economic-development arm that recruits and supports companies; Smart Columbus is the innovation lab that serves residents through technology and mobility pilots.

Where did Smart Columbus come from?

It launched after the region won the U.S. DOT Smart City Challenge and the matching private investment that followed, and it has continued to test, prove, and scale new ideas as a standing program of the Partnership.
